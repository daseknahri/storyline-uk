# Storyline UK — brand sheet

**Name:** Storyline UK
**Tagline:** Movies · TV · Streaming · Celebrity
**What it is:** UK entertainment news publication (MovieWeb-pattern, REA spec): movie news, TV news, streaming charts and launches, celebrity news, features & lists, trailers & videos — written for UK readers (UK release dates, UK streaming availability, UK broadcast times, British English). Planned domain: `storylineuk.getemoji.site` (free subdomain, Dales pattern — launch steps are owner-gated).

## Mark
A gold clapperboard on a deep-noir circular badge: the slate is striped carpet-red and gold, and the board's face carries a bold black play-button triangle — clapper + play geometry, the two shapes of film and streaming in one emblem. Original generated art; no copied logos, no real likenesses. Files: `icon-512.png` (site icon), `apple-touch-180.png`, `favicon-32.png`, `logo.png` (ink text, light backgrounds), `logo-light.png` (cream text, dark backgrounds). Type is composited (`brand_compose.py`, Anton), never baked by an image model.

## Palette
- Cinema noir (base): `#0B0D12` / soft `#12151D` / surface `#171B25`
- Carpet red (primary accent): `#D7263D`, bright derivative for text on dark `#FF5A6E`
- Premiere gold (secondary accent): `#E8A33D`
- Warm cream ink: `#F4F1EA`; muted `#A7AEBC`; line `#262C3A`
- Category moods: Movie News gold `#E8A33D` · TV News blue `#4EA8DE` · Streaming red `#FF5A6E` · Celebrity News violet `#C77DBB` · Features & Lists teal `#3FA7A0` · Trailers & Videos orange `#E2703A`
- Clearly distinct from Dales & Cobbles (green/brick/cream light scheme): this brand is dark-first with red/gold.

## Type
Headlines: **Anton** (bold condensed, uppercase — the network pattern). Body: system sans for news pace; Literata serif kept for excerpts/pull-quotes.

## Art
`hero-1920.jpg` — a grand dark cinema: crimson velvet seats, red-carpet aisle, golden screen glow and a projector beam (homepage hero). `og.jpg` — social card (hero + lockup). All art is original generated imagery: no studio stills, screenshots, video frames or publicity photos anywhere on the site; article imagery follows the network rule (own generated content only, iterated to the MovieWeb-grade bar — see the entertainment IMAGE-PLAYBOOK).

## Voice
Sharp, warm, fan-to-fan British entertainment writing. Facts rewritten in our own words from official announcements, trailers and reputable reporting (credited and linked); YouTube embeds for trailers; rumours always labelled; never fabricate quotes or claims about real people.
